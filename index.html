About KIET

Karachi Institute of Economics & Technology (KIET) is a higher education institution located in Karachi, Pakistan. It provides students with opportunities to develop academic knowledge, technical skills and professional experience.

Our goal is to prepare students for a successful future through education, creativity and innovation.
